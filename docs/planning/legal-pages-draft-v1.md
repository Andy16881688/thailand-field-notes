### What this site is

Thailand Field Notes is a practical travel guide for English-speaking first-time independent visitors who want clearer decisions about traveling in Thailand. It organizes useful information around real planning questions rather than asking readers to sort through an unstructured list of destinations.

### What we cover

The site covers trip length and route planning, entry and arrival basics, local and long-distance transport, destination comparisons, Thai food, and cultural etiquette. Content is arranged through Plan Your Trip, Entry Essentials, Getting Around, and Destinations & Local Context.

### How we create content

We aim for clear, readable guidance organized around real search intent and practical traveler tasks. Pages are reviewed for relevance, internal consistency, and useful connections to related topics. Information that can change, such as entry requirements, transport operations, weather, and opening conditions, should be checked against current official or provider sources.

### Important limitations

Thailand Field Notes provides general travel information. It is not a government tourism authority, travel agency, booking service, immigration adviser, lawyer, doctor, emergency service, or insurance provider. Nothing on the site guarantees entry, safety, availability, pricing, weather, or a particular travel outcome.

### Our reader promise

We will make planning topics easier to understand, avoid unnecessary jargon, separate practical guidance from opinion, keep pages readable on mobile, and connect readers to related resources when that helps them make a better decision.

### Contact and updates

Official contact information will be published when contact channels are enabled for this site. Pages may be corrected or updated when the site scope or important travel information changes.
